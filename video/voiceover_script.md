## Plain read-through (for rehearsal)

Starting university should be exciting. But on day one, you're registering for courses, finding a job, understanding visa rules, and opening tab after tab.

This is USC's own quick-links page: twelve different services. Billing, health, registration, events, each in its own portal. The answers are scattered.

So we built Trojan Compass. One assistant for your whole USC experience, one conversation away.

Here's a new MS Computer Science student. They ask, in plain English, for AI and machine-learning courses, prerequisites, and sections that fit together. A supervisor routes the question to our academic agent, and every fact is verified against USC data. Course cards show units, terms, prerequisites, and sources. Pick sections, and you see your week. Course planning with sources, not guesses.

Now research. Trojan Compass finds labs that match AI and software engineering, like INK and JAUNTS. It shows each lab's recruitment status exactly as published, so nobody mistakes an old post for an open position, and it tells you how to apply. Opportunities beyond the job boards.

For a new international student, the question is: what do I finish before the semester starts? Trojan Compass builds a checklist from verified USC guides, from payments to registration. Tick items off, and your progress is saved. One clear path.

Same prompt, two assistants. On the left, ChatGPT with web search: a capable answer from general sources. On the right, Trojan Compass: answers from verified USC records, each with a dated source, and shaped by the student's own profile.

Security is built in, not bolted on. A prompt-injection attempt is blocked before any model is called. One student can't open another student's session. Personal numbers, emails and unknown links are redacted, and our logs record events, never messages.

And it's efficient, with every number measured. The first answer took three model calls, for less than a tenth of a cent. Ask again, and it comes from the cache: zero live calls. Without USC data, the same model is simply ungrounded.

It works on your phone, adapts to each student's profile, and more languages, voice input and deadline alerts are next on our roadmap.

Public USC resources become structured knowledge, retrieved and checked by agents into a verified answer. It runs on public data, at a measured cost, with safety built in. One university. One intelligent companion. This is Trojan Compass.

## Recording tips
- Record in a quiet room, phone or USB mic 15–20 cm away. Do one take per scene so mistakes are cheap to redo.
- Save the takes as `video/remotion/public/audio/voiceover/s01.wav` … `s11.wav`. We'll sync them to the scene start times above.
- If a line runs long, cut adjectives rather than speeding up.
