# USC post-completion OPT

## Request then file
Confirm graduation eligibility, request an OPT I-20 from OIS through Trojan International, and receive it before submitting Form I-765 to USCIS. A job offer is not required to apply. Work must relate to the major.

## Watch both deadlines
The USC page lists a filing window from 90 days before program completion through the 60-day post-completion period, with additional timing requirements. OIS's latest OPT I-20 request is 45 days after completion. Do not treat an I-20 request as a USCIS filing. Check the complete instructions and current filing receipt deadlines before submitting.

## Employment start and reporting
Begin only after receiving the EAD and reaching its start date. Report employment and address updates through Trojan International. Standard post-completion OPT is up to 12 months; STEM extension eligibility and procedure are separate.

## Individual eligibility
One academic year in F-1 status and restrictions involving earlier OPT or full-time CPT apply. OIS should resolve individual degree-history or status questions.
