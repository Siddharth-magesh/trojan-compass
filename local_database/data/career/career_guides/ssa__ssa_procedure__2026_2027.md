# SSA application and document rules

## Start and complete the application
SSA directs applicants to start online and complete the office or card-center visit with documents within 45 calendar days. If online initiation is unavailable, use an SSA office. Bring original documents or copies certified by their issuer, not ordinary photocopies or notarized copies.

## Match evidence to status
F-1 campus workers need school-official evidence plus employer evidence. CPT students need the signed CPT I-20; EAD holders need the employment authorization document. J-1 applicants need sponsor authorization. Immigration status, identity, and age must be established.

## Timing is conditional
SSA cannot process a campus/CPT application when work starts more than 30 days away, or an EAD-based application before the EAD start date. Its generic school-reporting guidance does not replace USC's separate OIS letter process.
