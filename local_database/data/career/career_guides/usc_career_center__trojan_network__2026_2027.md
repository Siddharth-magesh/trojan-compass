# Trojan Network and alumni conversations

## Access and discover mentors
Create a profile through the official Trojan Network link. Public USC documentation describes Connect, then Network, with company, industry, and help-topic filters for alumni. Short informational conversations and longer formal mentoring are different options.

## Prepare a focused conversation
Collector guidance: introduce your USC program and a specific reason for contacting the person. Ask for insight into their work or path rather than an immediate referral. Review public background, prepare questions, respect the agreed time, and send a brief thank-you. Ask permission before forwarding advice or contact details.

## LinkedIn and legacy ViterbiLink
USC's networking guidance also points to LinkedIn for professional exploration. An older Viterbi guide mentions ViterbiLink, but its current endpoint could not be verified. Prefer the currently documented Trojan Network route and ask VCC about legacy access.

Sources: [USC networking](https://careers.usc.edu/channels/network/) and [Viterbi informational interviews](https://viterbicareers.usc.edu/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/Informational-Interview-Guide-1.pdf), checked November 14, 2026. Private profiles, messages, and contact exports are not part of this dataset.
