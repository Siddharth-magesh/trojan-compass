# SSN after an eligible campus job offer

## Employer and OIS steps
Obtain a dated employer-letterhead letter with passport name, employer/department, supervisor name/title/phone, job title/duties, start date, weekly hours, pay, EIN, and ink signature with typed signer name/title. USC's EIN is 95-1642394.

Submit the SSN Eligibility Letter Request in Trojan International. New arrivals wait 10 business days after US entry before requesting it; OIS states processing takes up to 4 business days. Collect the signed OIS letter.

## SSA appointment documents
Schedule an SSA appointment no more than 30 days before campus employment starts. Bring original employer and OIS letters, SS-5, passport/visa, I-20 or DS-2019, and latest I-94. For approved CPT, OIS says no separate OIS SSN letter is required; use the CPT I-20 and employer letter. For approved OPT, use the EAD and OPT documents.

## Authorization and onboarding are separate
SSNs support wage reporting; they do not grant permission to work. SSA does not require an SSN before starting authorized work, while USC's page uses stricter start-work wording. Confirm payroll onboarding with the hiring department and OIS.

Source: [SSA international-student guidance](https://www.ssa.gov/pubs/EN-05-10181.pdf), checked November 14, 2026.
