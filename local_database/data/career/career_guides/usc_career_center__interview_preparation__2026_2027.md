# USC interview and mock-interview resources

## Use Big Interview
Current students enter through USC Single Sign-On from the official resource page. Alumni obtain an organizational code from the Alumni Handshake resource library. Big Interview includes a question library, simulator, and feedback on recorded practice.

## Choose relevant practice
Browse by industry, career level, and competency. Practice aloud and review the feedback. For additional human help, use USC career advising and the Viterbi event calendar; mock interview events have their own registration.

## Collector technical-practice checklist
Practice explaining a project, assumptions, complexity, tests, and tradeoffs. Prepare truthful examples of collaboration and setbacks. For research interviews, explain a relevant paper and propose a small extension or replication. These practice suggestions do not predict an employer's actual interview format.
