# USC workshops, technical communities, and preparation

## Trojan Ready and employer events
Trojan Ready offers employer-led workshops in fall and spring. Select a future session from the official agenda and inspect its registration details. Older agenda entries remain historical, not upcoming. Prepare questions related to the employer and workshop topic.

## Engineering communities
Viterbi's student organization directory links technical, professional, and design groups. Verify the group's current membership and meeting information because the directory includes older-year labels. A student group is a skill-building resource, not employment.

Source: [Viterbi organizations](https://viterbiundergrad.usc.edu/student-organizations/), checked November 14, 2026.

## Training and credentials
The Viterbi toolkit links career-readiness and interview resources. Completion certificates should not be represented as accredited professional certifications. Collector guidance: use a project or practice activity to demonstrate a skill before paying for an unrelated credential.

Source: [Viterbi toolkit](https://viterbicareers.usc.edu/toolkit/), checked November 14, 2026.
