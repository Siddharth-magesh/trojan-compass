# Finding USC MS research and approaching faculty

## Choose the right route
An interest form means a lab accepts expressions of interest; it does not establish funding or a current project. Directed research is an academic route. An hourly student-worker role and a formal graduate RA appointment may have different eligibility. Read the exact announcement.

## Match the lab's instructions
INK asks for background, relevant research/programming experience, research fit, a time plan, and a PDF CV. Allegro and JAUNTS publish specific USC student forms. Use the requested route; do not substitute a generic mass email.

Sources: [Robin Jia](https://robinjia.github.io/) and [Jay Pujara](https://www.jaypujara.org/), checked November 14, 2026.

## Collector outreach checklist
Read a recent project or paper. Explain one concrete connection to your skills and propose a modest contribution. State degree level, availability, and intended duration accurately. Ask about current scope, supervision, compensation, credit, and authorization requirements before starting. No-opening or closed records should not be described as recruiting.
