# Engineering resume and application preparation

## Start from USC's toolkit
Use Viterbi's resume and cover-letter toolkit as the reference, then follow the employer's own requested materials and submission instructions. Arrange human feedback through Viterbi career services.

## Collector preparation checklist
For a software or ML role, select projects that demonstrate relevant languages, systems, datasets, and contributions. Describe your actual work and supported results; do not invent performance figures. Make repository links usable and remove secrets or restricted material. Keep a local copy of the job description, submission date, and requisition ID.

For research, emphasize methods, implementation, evaluation, and your specific contribution. If an employer asks for a cover letter, connect relevant work to that role. This checklist is editorial guidance, not a USC hiring rule.
