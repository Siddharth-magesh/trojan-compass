# USC tuition deadlines, late fees and account holds (2026-2027)

## Registration and settlement (payment) deadlines, late fees and finance charges

Register by this date so tuition appears on your bill before the settlement deadline: Fall 2026 - July 17, 2026; Spring 2027 - December 11, 2026. Settlement deadline (tuition and fees must be paid, deferred, or covered by a payment plan by 5 p.m. Pacific time to avoid late fees): Fall 2026 - August 21, 2026; Spring 2027 - January 8, 2027. Summer tuition and fees are assessed at registration and due as stated on the billing statement.

Late settlement fee: if tuition and fees are not paid or deferred by the settlement deadline, a $100 late fee is charged on each of three dates - Fall 2026: August 21, August 28 and September 4; Spring 2027: January 8, January 15 and January 22. A monthly finance charge applies to balances not paid by the monthly due date; the current annual rate is 12 percent. First day to request refunds of a credit balance: Fall 2026 - August 19, 2026; Spring 2027 - January 6, 2027. Agency billing authorization letters are due by each term's settlement deadline.

The health insurance waiver, meal plan changes and tuition refund insurance opt-out have their own deadlines.

## Monthly billing schedule (eBill statement dates and due dates)

Statement date -> due date: 3/14/26 -> 4/10/26; 4/11/26 -> 5/15/26; 5/16/26 -> 6/12/26; 6/13/26 -> 7/17/26; 7/18/26 -> 8/21/26 (fall tuition deadline); 8/22/26 -> 9/18/26; 9/19/26 -> 10/16/26; 10/17/26 -> 11/13/26; 11/14/26 -> 12/11/26; 12/12/26 -> 1/8/27 (spring tuition deadline); 1/9/27 -> 2/12/27; 2/13/27 -> 3/19/27; 3/20/27 -> 4/16/27; 4/17/27 -> 5/14/27; 5/15/27 -> 6/18/27. Unpaid balances on the tuition deadline dates (8/21/26, 1/8/27, 5/14/27) are subject to finance charges and late fees. Summer tuition and fees are due at enrollment regardless of session dates.

## What happens if I pay late (past-due balances)

Charges unpaid after the due date, or a payment rejected by your bank, make your account past due: late fees and finance charges are assessed and a hold is placed that restricts services such as registration and your diploma. To resolve: pay the balance through any payment option (sfs.usc.edu/payment/). If expected aid has not posted, contact the office responsible: Financial Aid (grants, scholarships, loans), your academic department (departmental awards such as TA/RA tuition remission), or Agency Billing (sponsor payments). Help: University Collections, 213-740-9087, collect@usc.edu.

Source page: https://sfs.usc.edu/student-account/past-due-balances/

## Account holds: what they mean and how to remove them

A hold on your student record can block registration, transcripts, diploma, USCard and housing. Check all holds in Experience USC under My Academics > Registration Information. Common holds: COLL 01/02/03/10 = past-due student account - pay in USCe.pay or contact University Collections, 213-740-9087, collect@usc.edu. COLL 11 = prepayment of tuition required before registration - contact University Collections. SFS 05 = past-due payment plan - log in to USCe.pay, open the payment plan and bring it current. LOAN 01 = past-due student loan - pay through Heartland ECSI (heartlandecsi.com) or contact SFS at 213-740-3490 / uscsfs@usc.edu. LOAN 03 = loan exit counseling required.

FAO 20 = Financial Aid Office hold - contact financialaid.usc.edu. Note: incomplete immunization records after the Student Health deadline also create a registration hold (handled in MySHR, not USCe.pay).

Source page: https://sfs.usc.edu/student-account/holds/
