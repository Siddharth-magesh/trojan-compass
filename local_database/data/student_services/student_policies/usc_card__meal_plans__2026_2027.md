# USC meal plans: dates, change deadlines and rules (2026-2027)

## Who needs a meal plan

Meal plans are provided by USC Hospitality and administered by USCard Services. Residents of USC Housing are automatically enrolled in a mandatory meal plan based on their housing location and class standing, and may upgrade to another plan online before the change deadline. Mandatory meal plans cannot be cancelled. Students not required to have a plan (for example, graduate students living off campus) may buy an optional plan online. Changes may create a charge or credit on your student account.


## Meal plan dates and change deadlines for 2026-2027

Fall 2026: meal plans run August 16, 2026 to December 16, 2026; the deadline to change your meal plan is September 2, 2026. Spring 2027: January 7, 2027 to May 12, 2027; the deadline to change is January 21, 2027. A plan starts on your housing move-in date, or on the purchase date for an optional plan, and runs to the end of the term.


## Unused meal plan funds

Meal plans end at the close of each semester, except Community 25 and Community 50 plans, which end on the last day of the school year. Remaining meal plan funds cannot be refunded, transferred or sold. Meal plan balances are separate from USCard discretionary funds, which do roll over.
