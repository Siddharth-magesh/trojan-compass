# USC Payment Plan and Flywire International Payment Plan (2026-2027)

## USC Payment Plan (pay in monthly installments): including the Flywire International Payment Plan

The USC Payment Plan lets students and approved guest users pay tuition, fees, university housing, meal plans and other university charges in interest-free installments over the term. You must apply again every semester. Eligibility: registered for classes for the upcoming term and student account current. Standard programs: a down payment plus four monthly installments (August-December or January-May). Trimester programs (e.g., Dental, Law, several online and professional master's programs): down payment plus three installments. No payment plan for summer. Application fee: $75 per plan, non-refundable.

Option 1 - USCe.pay Plan for U.S. bank accounts: log in to USCe.pay, select "Payment Plans," pay the $75 fee, make the down payment, and set up automatic deduction from a U.S. checking or savings account; deductions run on the 3rd of each month.

Option 2 - Flywire International Payment Plan (for paying from a non-U.S. bank): log in to Experience USC, choose "Flywire International Payment Plan" under Finances and Billing, select your plan, enter the amount to finance in "Balance for Plan," activate the plan, then arrange the first payment including the $75 fee; payment options depend on your country, and each installment must be completed by the 3rd of the month. Spring 2027 enrollment windows: International Trimester Plan 11/25/2026-2/2/2027; International Regular Plan 12/09/2026-2/2/2027; Transact (U.S.) Trimester Plan 11/14/2026-1/29/2027; Transact Regular Plan 12/14/2026-1/29/2027.

The plan is not adjusted automatically when charges change; to change or cancel, submit the Payment Plan Change Request Form (https://sfs.usc.edu/payment-plan-change-request-form/). USC can cancel a plan for missed payments. Questions: 213-740-4077, uscsfs@usc.edu.
