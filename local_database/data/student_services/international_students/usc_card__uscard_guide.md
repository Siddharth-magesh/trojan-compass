# USCard (USC student ID card) guide

## Getting your first USCard

The USCard is your USC photo ID. It belongs to USC, must be shown to university officials on request, is not transferable, and can be revoked for policy violations. Your first USCard is free; a replacement costs $25. To be eligible you must be registered for the current semester (or be an active employee or affiliate). You may hold only one ID; students who are also employees get a faculty/staff ID that includes student privileges.

To get your first card online (mycard.usc.edu > Get Your USCard > First ID - Upload Your Photo): 1) fill out the USC ID application; 2) securely upload your photo and a government or state issued photo ID (an international student can use a passport); 3) select your affiliation (student). You receive an email with the status of your submission.

Photo requirements: a recent, front-view color photo, unedited (no app filters), with space between the top of your head and the top of the image, no hats or sunglasses, maximum 10 MB.


## Adding money to your USCard (discretionary funds)

Money deposited to your USCard is called discretionary funds: a prepaid declining balance, separate from meal plan dining dollars, accepted anywhere on campus and at select USC Village locations. Unused balance rolls over to the next term.

Deposit online with a credit or debit card (Visa, Mastercard or Discover) through the USCard eAccounts portal (+Add Money), or in person at a USCard customer service center. Deposits made through your student account appear as a charge on your USC bill.

Refunds: discretionary deposits are not refundable until you graduate or withdraw; any remaining balance is then credited to your student account. If a registered student's account has no transactions for 12 months, the balance is refunded to the student billing account. When graduating or withdrawing, email uscard@usc.edu to close the account and request the refund.


## Managing your USCard online (eAccounts portal and app)

Log in at mycard.usc.edu > Log In to Your USCard Account > Students > Login To Your USCard with your USC NetID (this opens the Transact eAccounts portal at usc-sp.transactcampus.com). Students can also use the USCard mobile app for iOS and Android. Online you can: check your balance and transaction history, deposit funds, manage meal plans, view accepted locations, freeze the card if lost or stolen, and reactivate it if found.

Portal menu: Accounts (Account Summary shows each account such as Student Debit with its balance and a +Add Money button; Account Transactions lets you search purchases by date range, amount range and location; Board Transactions shows meal plan swipes and dining dollars); Card Services (Deactivate Card, Activate Card); Profile (Personal Information, Alert Preferences, Saved Payment Methods); and Contact Us (a message form). To receive email alerts for card activity, low balance or deposits, first add an email address in Profile > Personal Information, then choose alerts in Alert Preferences.


## Lost, stolen, damaged or replacement USCard

If your card is lost or stolen, log in to the eAccounts portal and use Card Services > Deactivate Card to freeze it; it stays deactivated until you reactivate it (Card Services > Activate Card) if you find it. While frozen, your funds and building door access do not work. If you have no online access, call USCard Services at (213) 740-8709 (Monday-Friday, 9 a.m.-5 p.m.) or, after hours, the Department of Public Safety at (213) 740-6000.

A replacement card costs $25; request it online. If the card was stolen, provide a police report and the $25 fee is waived. Cards worn or damaged by normal use are replaced free at a customer service center (bring the old card). Once a replacement is issued the old card is permanently deactivated and its services transfer to the new card.


## If your USCard does not work

In University Housing, go to your building's housing customer service center. In other university buildings, contact that building's facility manager. At USC fitness centers, ask Recreational Sports. For any other card problem, email uscard@usc.edu or call (213) 740-8709; after business hours call DPS at (213) 740-6000.
