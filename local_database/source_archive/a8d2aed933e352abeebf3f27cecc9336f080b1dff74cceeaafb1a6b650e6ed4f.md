# MySHR (Student Health portal) guide

## What is MySHR (My Student Health Record)

MySHR is the USC Student Health patient portal at https://usc.edu/myshr (login page https://eshc-pncw.usc.edu/). Use it to: upload immunization records (Medical Records), complete consent for treatment (Consent Forms), update phone numbers and emergency contact (Profile), send secure messages (Messages) to the Advice Nurse for non-urgent questions, your established medical/counseling/psychiatry provider, or a confidential advocate for sexual assault or gender-based harm, book workshops and "Let's Talk" sessions, schedule appointments (provider visits, immunizations, labs; same-day/next-day illness or injury slots are often available), submit a Counseling Group Interest Form, view medical records, clinical notes and lab results, and submit or check the health insurance waiver (Insurance Waiver/Enrollment tab).

Source page: https://sites.usc.edu/new-student-health-requirements/home/myshr-quickstart-guide/

## How to log in to MySHR for the first time

Step 1: Activate your USC NetID at https://itservices.usc.edu/uscnetid (MySHR will not work until it is active) and check your USC email regularly. Step 2: Wait - MySHR access should be available within 48 hours after the NetID becomes active. Step 3: Go to https://usc.edu/myshr and sign in with your NetID. If you cannot log in, take a screenshot of the error and email it, with your full name, date of birth and 10-digit USC ID number (shown on your USC ID card), to eshchim@usc.edu (Student Health Information Management). Never share your USC login with anyone, including family: it gives access to private health information.

Source page: https://sites.usc.edu/new-student-health-requirements/home/myshr-quickstart-guide/

## MySHR portal: menu map (what each sidebar section is for)

After logging in to MySHR (usc.edu/myshr), the Home page shows Quick Access buttons (Schedule an Appointment, Send a Secure Message, View Health Clearances, View My Medical Records, View My Account Summary), your upcoming appointments, and an Immunization Status summary (Measles, Meningococcal ACWY, Mumps, Varicella, Tuberculosis Testing, HPV) marked Compliant or Incomplete. Left sidebar sections: MY PROFILE - Profile (contact info, emergency contact), Insurance Waiver/Enrollment, Insurance Card, Account Summary (Student Health charges). MY CARE - Appointments, Medical Clearances, Referrals, Outside Orders, Consent Forms, Surveys, Messages (secure inbox), Letters. MY HEALTH - Health History, Immunizations (list of recorded vaccine and TB test events), Medical Records, Visit Summary.

Other - Groups/Workshops, Handouts (CDC vaccine information statements), Supplemental Forms (exemption and special forms). Log Out is at the bottom and in the account menu. The 24/7 contact center (213-740-9355) can connect you to an advice nurse, help book appointments, answer questions about requirements and insurance, and connect you to an on-call crisis counselor or confidential advocate; you can also chat via studenthealth.usc.edu or email studenthealth@usc.edu for non-urgent questions. Emergencies: call 911; on campus DPS 213-740-4321 (UPC) or 323-442-1000 (HSC).

## MySHR Medical Clearances page: how to check if you are cleared and fix missing items

Open MySHR > Medical Clearances. At the top is your Overall Clearance Status (Satisfied or Not Satisfied). The table "Items required for clearance" lists: Consents - Financial Responsibility and Student Consent to Treatment; Immunizations - Immunization Record, Measles, Meningococcal [ACWY], Mumps, Varicella; TB Screening - Tuberculosis Testing; Other - Health History Form. Each row shows a status (Compliant / Not Compliant) and Details. Items with an Update button let you enter vaccine dates or test results. A second table lists items NOT required for clearance (e.g., HPV).

Rules shown on the page: to upload vaccine or lab documents from outside Student Health, click Update on the Immunization Record row; if you type in vaccine dates or lab results you must also upload a scan or photo of the documentation, and submissions with missing, invalid or incomplete documentation are rejected. Do NOT add vaccines or lab tests you received at USC Student Health or USC Pharmacies - those are added to your record automatically. To request a medical, religious or philosophical exemption, use the form in Supplemental Forms; status updates after review.

If you have not registered for classes yet, check back two business days after registering or after any registration change.

Source page: https://eshc-pncw.usc.edu/Clearances

## Why was my immunization or TB record marked non-compliant in MySHR

Student Health reviews each upload and sends the result as a secure message in MySHR > Messages, and the Contact Center also sends "Your Immunization Compliance Status" messages. Common rejection reasons seen in these messages: the uploaded immunization record has no name or date of birth on it (every page must show your full name and DOB), and TB screening is non-compliant because no acceptable test was submitted - only a TB blood test (IGRA, e.g., T-Spot or QuantiFERON) is accepted, and prior tests count only if done at a U.S. lab within 6 months of classes starting.

To fix it: get a corrected or translated record with your name and DOB, then upload it again through Medical Clearances > Update on the Immunization Record row; for TB, book a TB blood test in MySHR (Appointments > Immunizations) or at the Immunization Fair in the first week of classes. Tests done at Student Health appear automatically and turn the item Compliant.

Source page: https://eshc-pncw.usc.edu/Clearances

## How to book an appointment in MySHR (categories and visit types)

Go to MySHR > Appointments > Schedule an Appointment, choose a category, click Continue, then choose a visit type and time slot. Categories: Medical; Sexually Transmitted Infection (STI) Testing; Immunizations; Counseling and Mental Health; Confidential Advocacy, Resources, and Education Support Center (CARE-SC) for students who have experienced relationship or sexual violence; Laboratory or X-Ray (existing orders only).

Medical visit types: Cold / Flu / Cough / Fever / Sore Throat (if you are sick or need to be seen today); Sexual and Reproductive Health; Urinary Tract Infection (female; males choose Sexual and Reproductive Health); Secure Message Provider Consult (acne treatment or contraception); General (all other reasons); Specialty (physical therapy, sports medicine, nutrition counseling, occupational therapy, acne, gender-affirming care); Medication Refills; Referral; Advice Nurse (if unsure whether you need to be seen). Eating concern, allergy, chiropractic, acupuncture and orthopedics appointments must be booked by phone at 213-740-9355. Some mental health appointments cannot be cancelled online; call 213-740-9355. You can enable text message appointment reminders on the Appointments page.

Source page: https://eshc-pncw.usc.edu/Mvc/AppointmentsHome

## MySHR Insurance Waiver/Enrollment tab: how to waive or enroll in SHIP/Aetna

Open MySHR > Insurance Waiver/Enrollment. Review the USC Student Health Insurance Requirements (linked) first. Option 1 WAIVE ("I have my own insurance") - use it if your plan meets USC's minimum coverage requirements and you want to opt out of SHIP/Aetna. Option 2 ENROLL ("Enroll me into SHIP/Aetna") - for students who are not automatically enrolled but want SHIP. PhD students, international students, health professions students and students with 6 or more credits are enrolled automatically and need to do nothing to KEEP SHIP/Aetna. Online-program students who join SHIP pay a higher premium because they do not get primary care through the Student Health Fee.

The page lists your previously submitted waiver requests with the academic year, the deadline (e.g., 2026-2027 waiver deadline 9/11/2026, plan year 08/15/2026-08/14/2027) and the decision (Approved / Denied / Pending). A waiver must be submitted again every academic year.

Source page: https://eshc-pncw.usc.edu/Mvc/WaiveEnroll

## MySHR Insurance Card section (outside insurance only)

MySHR > Insurance Card is only for students with outside (non-USC) insurance. Students on SHIP/Aetna do not upload a card here; they get their card from the Aetna Student Health portal or app. Uploading an outside card does NOT count as a waiver of SHIP - the waiver is a separate step in Insurance Waiver/Enrollment. Click Add New Card and upload images of the front and back (accepted file types: PNG, GIF, JPEG, JPG).

Source page: https://eshc-pncw.usc.edu/Mvc/InsuranceCards

## MySHR Account Summary: Student Health charges and how to pay them

MySHR > Account Summary shows your Total Patient Balance and a statement for each visit (date of service, provider, statement number, charges, insurance payment, balance). Most Student Health visits have no charge, but items such as immunizations, some labs and copays can create charges. Click Pay Balance to pay in MySHR, or View/Print to print a walkout statement for a visit. You have 5 business days to pay through the patient portal; after that the charges post to your USC student account and appear in USCe.pay (Pay My Bill).

Source page: https://eshc-pncw.usc.edu/StatementsHome

## MySHR Supplemental Forms (vaccine exemptions, release of information, special programs)

MySHR > Supplemental Forms lets you download a form, complete it, upload it and click Save at the bottom of the page.

Forms: Declaration of Religious or Philosophical Objection for Required Vaccinations; Medical Exemption for Required Vaccinations (both for student compliance requirements only, not flu or COVID-19); Release of Information (only when your provider instructs you; upload with a copy of government ID and message your provider); HSC LAGMC E2 TB history form (health professions students needing LA General Medical Center access); Summer Program Consent (summer program participants only); TB Screening Questionnaire for DC Program students; Trojan Safety Plan and Interpersonal Violence Safety Plan (only if instructed by your mental health provider).

Other MySHR sections: Consent Forms lists forms you have signed (e.g., Financial Responsibility, vaccine screening at the Immunization Fair or flu clinic); Handouts holds CDC Vaccine Information Statements for vaccines you received; Groups/Workshops lists bookable workshops by theme (ADHD & neurodiversity, alcohol & substance use, anxiety & stress, depression & mood, identity & belonging - including an F1/J1 Graduate Support group for international students - life skills, and healthy relationships).

Source page: https://eshc-pncw.usc.edu/Mvc/DownloadableForms
