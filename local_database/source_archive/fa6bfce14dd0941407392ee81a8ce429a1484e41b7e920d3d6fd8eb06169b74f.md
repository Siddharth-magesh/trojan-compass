# F-1 and J-1 campus employment

## Work limits and enrollment
OIS permits eligible F-1/J-1 degree students to start campus work in their first semester. The campus-work limit is 20 hours weekly during fall/spring and up to 40 during summer and official breaks, combined across campus jobs. Payroll enrollment minimums are 6 undergraduate, 4 master's, and 3 doctoral units; these are not immigration full-time enrollment definitions. Final-semester and thesis exceptions require the specified USC procedures.

## Work-study and authorization
Federal Work-Study is unavailable to international students. An SSN does not itself authorize work. Check job eligibility with OIS; a location near campus does not establish on-campus employment.

## Summer payroll qualification
Employee Gateway generally limits student work to 20 hours during enrolled sessions, including summer, and allows more during breaks or summer without classes. Confirm both payroll and immigration requirements before increasing hours.

Source: [USC student employees](https://employees.usc.edu/work-life/workplace-information/student-employees/), checked November 14, 2026.

## J-1 sponsor requirements
J-1 students should confirm sponsor authorization before employment. SSA requires a sponsor letter with an original signature authorizing the work; off-campus Academic Training is a separate route.

Sources: [SSA international students](https://www.ssa.gov/pubs/EN-05-10181.pdf) and [USC J-1 employment](https://ois.usc.edu/employment/j1-student-employment/), checked November 14, 2026.
