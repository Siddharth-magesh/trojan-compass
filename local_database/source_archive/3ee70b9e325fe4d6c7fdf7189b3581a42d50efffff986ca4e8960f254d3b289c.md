# USC CPT application and restrictions

## Confirm curricular eligibility first
The checked OIS page says CPT must be integral and required within the curriculum and related to the major. It explicitly excludes optional internships and elective courses. Confirm applicability with OIS and the academic internship coordinator before accepting a start date; do not rely on older generic advice.

## Request authorization
Secure the qualifying offer; follow program course-registration instructions. Submit the CPT I-20 request, offer letter, and cooperative agreement through Trojan International, using Viterbi's route when applicable. The academic advisor/coordinator approves before OIS issues the CPT I-20.

## Start only when authorized
OIS lists four business days for issuance at its authorization step. Review the employer and dates on the new I-20. Work cannot begin until the I-20 is received and its start date arrives. Unpaid internships also require the appropriate authorization.

## Protect later OPT eligibility
Twelve months or more of full-time CPT at the same degree level removes OPT eligibility at that level. Part-time CPT does not have that effect.
