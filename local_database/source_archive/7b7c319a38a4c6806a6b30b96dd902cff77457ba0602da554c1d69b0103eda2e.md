# How to pay USC tuition: accepted methods and international wire transfers

## Accepted ways to pay tuition (credit cards are NOT accepted)

Since July 1, 2020, USC does not accept credit or debit cards for tuition, fees, or other student account charges (California law prohibits private universities from charging card convenience fees).

Accepted methods: (1) Electronic check/ACH from a U.S. checking or savings account through USCe.pay; (2) the USC Payment Plan (interest-free installments); (3) pay by mail with a check payable to "University of Southern California" sent to University of Southern California, 620 West McCarthy Way, Suite 21, Los Angeles, CA 90089; (4) wire transfer through Flywire or Convera (domestic or international); (5) pay in person by check, cashier's check or money order drawn on a U.S. bank; plus 529 plans, prepayment, employer reimbursement and sponsoring agencies.

For international students, Flywire accepts foreign bank transfers, UnionPay, Alipay and other local methods, may save on exchange rates and lets you pay in your own currency.

Source page: https://sfs.usc.edu/updated-forms-of-payment/

## How international students pay tuition from abroad (Flywire / Convera wire transfer)

USC partners with Flywire and Convera for domestic and international tuition wire payments. USC charges no fee for using them, but your local bank may charge a fee to send the transfer. Wires take 2 to 5 business days after your bank initiates them, so send early enough to arrive before the settlement deadline. Only wire the amount needed for tuition and university fees; do not send money meant for other expenses such as a private landlord.

Paying by Flywire: (1) go to USCe.pay; (2) select "Make Payment"; (3) select "Tuition and Fees"; (4) verify the amount and click "Checkout"; (5) you are sent to the Flywire site; follow its instructions and complete payment within two business days; (6) the payment is credited to your student account once USC's bank receives the funds. Step-by-step Flywire video guides exist for payments from China and from India. Paying by Convera: go to the USC-specific Convera site https://students.convera.com/geo-buyer/usc2, follow the instructions, complete payment within two business days; it is credited once USC's bank receives the funds.

CIBC International Student Pay was discontinued effective October 30, 2026 and is no longer an option. Overpayment by wire: USC either returns the entire wire to the originating bank or you leave the excess on your account for future charges; partial refunds are not allowed.
