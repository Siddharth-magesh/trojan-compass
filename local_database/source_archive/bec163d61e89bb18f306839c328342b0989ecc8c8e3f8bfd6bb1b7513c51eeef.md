# USC Student Financial Services contacts and new-student finance checklist

## SFS new student financial checklist

Every USC student has a student account that reflects nearly all financial transactions at the university.

SFS recommends new students: (1) log in to USCe.pay for the first time; (2) set up guest users for online payments; (3) view upcoming payment deadlines; (4) learn about payment options; (5) consider a payment plan or prepayment; (6) complete Title IV authorizations in USCe.pay if receiving federal aid; (7) sign the master promissory note for student loans if borrowing; (8) sign up for direct deposit refunds; (9) make sure your taxpayer ID (SSN or ITIN) is on your student record; (10) learn about common fees; (11) find out whether you are eligible to waive USC student health insurance; (12) review the Paying for College orientation brochure; (13) set up agency billing if you are sponsored.

Source page: https://sfs.usc.edu/?p=343
