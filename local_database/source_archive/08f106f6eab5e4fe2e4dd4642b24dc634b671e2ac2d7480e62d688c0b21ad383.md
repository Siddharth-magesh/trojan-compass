# New international student journey through USCe.pay and MySHR

## Step-by-step: what a new international student must do in USCe.pay and MySHR

Before arrival: 1) Activate your USC NetID (itservices.usc.edu/uscnetid). 2) Log in to Experience USC > Finances & Billing > Pay My Bill (USCe.pay). 3) Invite parents/sponsors as USCe.pay guest users (My Account > Payers > Send a payer invitation). 4) Register for classes before the registration date so tuition appears on your bill. 5) Check your eBill (emailed to your @usc.edu address); it will include tuition, fees, the Student Health Fee and the SHIP/Aetna premium (international students are auto-enrolled). 6) Pay before the settlement deadline (5 p.m. Pacific): from abroad use Flywire through USCe.pay (Make Payment > Tuition and Fees > Checkout) or Convera; wires take 2-5 business days.

Credit cards are not accepted. Or enroll in the Flywire International Payment Plan ($75 fee). 7) In MySHR (available about 48 hours after NetID activation): sign the consent for treatment, upload MMR/varicella/meningococcal records with English translations, check whether TB blood screening applies to you, and update your emergency contact. 8) Decide on insurance: keep SHIP (most international students) or waive only if your plan meets all USC and U.S. visa criteria, by the Friday of week 3.

After arrival: 9) Open a U.S. bank account and set up direct deposit in USCe.pay (My Account) for refunds. 10) Get the TB blood test or any missing vaccines through MySHR appointments or the Immu-Fair in week one. 11) Download your Aetna card and save 213-740-9355. 12) Add your SSN/ITIN in Experience USC if you get one. Missing payments or immunizations both lead to registration holds.
