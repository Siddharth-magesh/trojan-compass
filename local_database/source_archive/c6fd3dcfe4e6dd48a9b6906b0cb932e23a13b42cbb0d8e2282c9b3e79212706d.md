# USC student bill, common fees, refunds and financial responsibility

## Understanding your eBill (billing statement) and what the terms mean

When you have a balance or account activity, USC emails a notice to your official @usc.edu address and to your guest users that a new eBill is available in USCe.pay. eBills are generated every four to five weeks; USC does not mail paper statements.

If you did not get the email: confirm the account actually has a balance or recent activity; remember notices go only to your official @usc.edu account, not a secondary departmental account; check that any email forwarding address is correct; check your guest user's email; add epayret@usc.edu to your address book so it is not marked spam; check email filters; and disable browser pop-up blockers if the eBill will not open. Terms: Previous Minimum Due = minimum due on the last statement. Pending Aid/Deferments = approved but not yet disbursed scholarships, grants, loans or deferments, temporarily subtracted from the balance. Due Date = deadline for currently billed items.

Minimum Due = least you must pay by the due date. Finance Charge section = how any finance charge was calculated (previous balance minus credits/payments). Agency Billing = amounts billed to a sponsor that are not yet paid; you must pay if the sponsor does not. Payment Plan = your plan budget and changes; the payment plan is a separate account under "Payment Plans" in USCe.pay.

## Common fees on a USC student account (what each charge means)

Tuition and fees vary by program, campus and units; current rates are in the Schedule of Classes (classes.usc.edu). Commitment deposit: nonrefundable, credited to the account after you register; graduate students with questions contact Graduate Admissions. New Student Fee: charged to first-time students on UPC, HSC or DTLA campuses; funds Welcome Week. Health insurance (SHIP/Aetna): international students are automatically enrolled regardless of units (domestic students at 6+ units); students with qualifying insurance may waive. Student Health Fee: required of all campus students, cannot be waived, separate from insurance. Housing and meal plans: billed to the account if you use USC housing or dining.

Late fees and finance charges: unpaid charges after the settlement deadline, including bounced payments, make the account past due and can create holds that block registration. Late registration fee: for registering after the settlement deadline. Norman H. Topping Student Aid Fee: mandatory semester fee funding scholarships for low-income students. Returned item fee: $25 for a returned check/EFT or a disputed card transaction. Student programming fee: all graduate students pay a graduate programming fee each semester regardless of units; undergraduates pay at 6+ units.

Transportation Services Fee: charged to all students attending classes in the LA area; funds the Lyft program, LAX buses during selected breaks and a free LA Metro U-Pass. Tuition refund insurance: automatic each term (can be waived); refunds tuition if you withdraw from all classes for illness or injury. USCard deposits and athletic ticket purchases can also appear as charges. Parking: transnet.usc.edu.

Source page: https://sfs.usc.edu/student-account/common-fees/

## How to get a refund of a credit balance (direct deposit)

If your account has a credit balance you may be eligible for a refund. Log in to USCe.pay and sign up for direct deposit under "My Account." If the bill was paid by cash, check, web check or financial aid, eligible refunds go by direct deposit into the student's bank account regardless of who paid, within 2-3 business days; confirm the funds are in your account before spending. You will need a bank account (international students usually open a U.S. account after arrival). Exceptions: if you have an active payment plan, a budget change (Payment Plan Change Request Form) is required before any refund.

If the overpayment came by wire transfer, USC either returns the entire wire to the originating bank or keeps the excess for future charges; partial refunds are not allowed. Parent PLUS loan refunds go to the parent borrower in 5-7 business days. Stipends/ad hoc payments direct deposit in USCe.pay is for domestic students. Earliest refund request dates: Fall 2026 - August 19, 2026; Spring 2027 - January 6, 2027.

Source page: https://sfs.usc.edu/refunds/

## Student Financial Responsibility Agreement (FRA)

All students must accept the Financial Responsibility Agreement to be enrolled at USC. By registering for classes you enter a binding agreement to pay the associated tuition and fees. The FRA covers payment expectations, non-payment, collections, communications, financial aid and university business processes. You remain responsible for your account even if a sponsor, employer or university aid is expected to pay, including any amount a third party fails to pay, and for charges they do not cover such as parking tickets, health center charges or housing damages.

Source page: https://sfs.usc.edu/student-account/usc-fra/

## Sponsored students (government / company scholarship): agency billing

If a company or government agency sponsors you (common for international students on government scholarships), all or part of your bill can be invoiced directly to the sponsor through Agency Billing. The sponsor's authorization letter must be submitted by each term's settlement deadline. Amounts billed to the agency appear on your eBill under Agency Billing; you are still responsible if the agency does not pay. Contact Agency Billing: 213-740-0427, agbill@usc.edu. More: https://sfs.usc.edu/agency/

Source page: https://sfs.usc.edu/payment/sponsoring-agencies/

## Prepaying tuition for multiple years

Admitted students in flat-fee programs can prepay 2 to 5 years of full-time tuition and fees, locking in current rates. Minimum is two years of tuition and mandatory fees. Steps: complete the prepayment inquiry form (sfs.usc.edu/prepayment-inquiry/); a Prepayment Agreement is emailed for signature; return it with payment by the settlement deadline of the first term covered. Unused funds may be refunded after graduation or departure: email uscsfs@usc.edu with subject "Request Prepayment Refund" or call 213-740-4077.

Source page: https://sfs.usc.edu/payment/prepayment/
