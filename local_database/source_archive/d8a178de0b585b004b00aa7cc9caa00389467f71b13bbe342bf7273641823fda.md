# USC campus job search

## Search and apply
Sign into USC Handshake. Open Jobs, All filters, then On Campus Student Employment under Job Type. Read the employer's requirements and application route. The Career Center lists year-round hiring and historically heavier fall posting activity in early August; that pattern is not a promise of a vacancy.

## Evaluate a posting
Check work-study requirements, student level, schedule, location, and compensation before applying. International students are not eligible for federal work-study positions. A department directory or generic interest form is a resource, not an open job.

## Libraries use Handshake
USC Libraries says all student hiring moved to Handshake on December 1, 2025. Do not use older library job portals or contact library employees to seek vacancies. Apply to a posted role; the hiring manager contacts selected applicants.

Source: [USC Libraries hiring instructions](https://libraries.usc.edu/jobs/library-jobs-students), checked November 14, 2026.
