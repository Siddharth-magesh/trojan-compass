# Using Viterbi career advising and Quinncia

## Book the correct service
In Handshake select Career Center, Appointments, Schedule a new appointment, then USC Viterbi Career Connections. Choose the topic and meeting method. In-person guidance lists RTH 218 and asks students to bring a laptop.

## Resume and cover-letter review
For the fall semester, use drop-ins for resume and cover-letter review. Read the toolkit and submit the resume to Quinncia beforehand. Join the official waitlist; availability can end early when capacity is reached. Check the source for closures rather than relying on a repeating weekly schedule.

## Internship-course questions
General career advising does not handle internship-course or D-clearance questions. Use the separately linked internship-course advising route.

## Prepare a useful session
Bring a target posting, current resume, and focused questions about fit, project descriptions, or interview preparation. This preparation checklist is collector guidance, not an appointment requirement.
