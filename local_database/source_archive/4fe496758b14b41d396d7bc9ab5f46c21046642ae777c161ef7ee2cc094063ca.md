# Getting care at USC Student Health: urgent help, locations and contacts

## New student health requirements checklist (what to finish in MySHR before classes)

Complete these before arriving on campus: (1) activate your NetID and log in to MySHR; (2) sign the consent for treatment in MySHR > Consent Forms; (3) upload immunization records (MMR, varicella, meningococcal ACWY if 21 or under) in MySHR > Medical Records; (4) international students: complete TB screening if required for your country; (5) decide on health insurance - keep SHIP/Aetna (automatic for international students) or submit a waiver by the deadline; (6) update your emergency contact in MySHR > Profile; (7) save the 24/7 Student Health number 213-740-9355 in your phone; (8) once classes start, print or download your Aetna insurance card.

Health services at Student Health (primary care, illness and injury, dermatology, sexual health, counseling, medication management, prevention education) are offered on campus and by telehealth with no copays and no insurance billing for visits, because they are funded by the Student Health Fee.

## How to book an appointment or get urgent help

Book in MySHR > Appointments (provider consultations, immunizations, labs). Each operating day some same-day/next-day appointments are reserved for illness or injury; book an illness/injury appointment in MySHR, and if none are available call Student Health at 213-740-9355. The 24/7 line 213-740-9355 (WELL) connects to an Advice Nurse, mental health counseling support and confidential advocates even when the centers are closed. Non-urgent medical questions: secure message to the Advice Nurse in MySHR > Messages. Emergencies: call 911. Department of Public Safety 24/7: 213-740-4321 (University Park Campus), 323-442-1000 (Health Sciences Campus). Therapy Plus offers telehealth therapy, chat support and self-paced tools.

If you take long-term medication (e.g., psychiatric or ADHD medication), bring at least a one-month supply because a first Student Health appointment can take 2-4 weeks; for chronic conditions, message the Advice Nurse in MySHR or email studenthealth@usc.edu with your USC ID, full name and date of birth.

Email: studenthealth@usc.edu. Current hours: https://studenthealth.usc.edu/hours/. Student Health Insurance Office: 213-821-1550 (direct), studenthealth@usc.edu, Monday-Thursday 8:30 a.m.-5 p.m., Friday 9:30 a.m.-5 p.m. MySHR login problems / medical records corrections: eshchim@usc.edu, 213-740-0206. Interpreter services are available on request. USC Pharmacy can fill prescriptions from Student Health or outside providers. Health records are confidential under HIPAA and FERPA.
