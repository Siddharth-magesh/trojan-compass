# External internships and research programs

## Search with a target
Use Handshake and the platform records for LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor, WayUp, Simplify, RippleMatch, and Wellfound. Collector guidance: search titles such as software engineering intern, research intern, ML intern, or data science intern, then inspect each employer's original requisition. Save its ID and deadline if stated.

## Check graduate eligibility explicitly
DOE SULI can include graduate enrollees through its recent-undergraduate-graduate route. VFP has a faculty-invited graduate route. SCGSR targets doctoral research. Their eligibility restrictions are different; do not treat every DOE program as an MS internship.

Sources: [SULI](https://science.osti.gov/wdts/suli/Eligibility), [VFP](https://science.osti.gov/wdts/vfp/Eligibility), [SCGSR](https://science.osti.gov/wdts/scgsr/Eligibility), checked November 14, 2026.

## Distinguish funding from a job
NSF GRFP supports eligible research-based graduate study. It is a fellowship, not an advertised internship. Read the current solicitation for citizenship, enrollment, field, and timing restrictions.

Source: [NSF GRFP](https://www.nsf.gov/funding/opportunities/grfp-nsf-graduate-research-fellowship-program), checked November 14, 2026.
