# USCe.pay (Pay My Bill) portal guide

## What is USCe.pay and what can I do in it

USCe.pay is USC's secure online system for managing your student account, run by Student Financial Services (SFS). It is the fastest and simplest way to pay your bill. In USCe.pay a student can: check the account balance, confirm the due date, review billing statements (eBills) and recent activity, create ACH payment profiles, pay by web check or wire transfer, enroll in a payment plan, sign up for direct deposit to receive refunds automatically, set up guest users (parents or sponsors) who can view and pay, and select Title IV authorization preferences (for federal aid recipients).

Student loan payments cannot be made in USCe.pay; the loan servicer (Heartland ECSI) has its own payment options.

Source page: https://sfs.usc.edu/epay/

## How to log in to USCe.pay for the first time

Step 1: Activate your USC NetID (your USC email username) at https://itservices.usc.edu/uscnetid if you have not already. Step 2: Log in to Experience USC at https://experience.usc.edu. Step 3: Open the Finances & Billing menu, where USCe.pay and Payment Plan are listed. Step 4: Select the "Pay My Bill (USCe.pay)" service. Parents and other third parties do not get Experience USC access; they log in through the guest pages after the student invites them.

Source page: https://sfs.usc.edu/epay/

## Finding Pay My Bill, Flywire plan and other finance links in Experience USC

In Experience USC (experience.usc.edu), the home page shows My Favorite Student Resources; Pay My Bill and MySHR are favorites by default. All finance links are under Student Resources > Finances & Billing: 1098T (ECSI) tax form; Financial Aid Services; Financial Literacy (iGrad); Flywire International Payment Plan; Pay My Bill (USCe.pay - account management, billing and payment, opens in a new tab); Registration Fee & Summary (summary of enrolled courses, tuition and fees); Student Financial Services; USCard Services. MySHR and Student Health are under Health & Emergencies. You can favorite 6 to 24 resources to show on your home page.

Note: starting November 19, 2026, USC sign-in is two steps - enter NetID@usc.edu first, then your password on the next screen (password and multifactor authentication stay the same).

Source page: https://experience.usc.edu/s/student-resources

## USCe.pay (Transact/CashNet) portal: menu map

Clicking Pay My Bill opens USCe.pay, hosted by Transact (CashNet). Left menu: My Account, Overview, Activity Details, Make a Payment, Transactions, Statements, Tax Forms, Help, Sign Out. Overview shows your balance, a Tuition and Fees Summary (Account Status such as Current, Due Date, Minimum Due, Last Statement Balance, Account Balance), "View statements" and "View activity details" links, a "Continue setting up your profile" box (Title IV authorization, direct deposit sign-up) and Recent transactions (date, description, receipt number, amount; e.g., the non-refundable commitment deposit and Tuition and Fees payments).

Make a Payment shows what you can pay; if you owe nothing it says "Nothing is available for payments right now." Statements holds your eBills. Help lists the contact email uscsfs@usc.edu.

## USCe.pay My Account: payment methods, invite a payer (parent), direct deposit refunds, Title IV

USCe.pay > My Account contains: Basic information; Payment methods - "Add payment method" to save a U.S. bank account for ACH payments; Payers - "Send a payer invitation" to give a parent or sponsor access so they can view and pay your bill; Direct deposit refunds - "Sign up" under Student Account Refunds so refunds of credit balances go to your bank account faster (a separate Stipends/Ad Hoc Payments sign-up is for domestic students); Title IV Funds Authorization - federal aid (Pell, SEOG, Direct Loans, PLUS) can only pay tuition, mandatory fees and room and board unless you authorize it to pay other charges such as books, parking, USCard and health center charges (most international students have no Title IV aid and can ignore this); SMS notifications sign-up; and Privacy & terms.

Source page: https://commerce.cashnet.com/cashneti/static/epayment/USCPAY/profile

## How to give parents or sponsors access (guest users)

Only the student can grant parents or other third parties access to the student account. There are two guest account types. (A) USCe.pay guest account: financial information only. To set it up, the student goes to the My Account page in USCe.pay and clicks "Send a payer invitation" under Payers. The guest then logs in at https://commerce.cashnet.com/uscpay?LT=P, where USCe.pay and Payment Plan are on the My Services page. (B) OASIS guest account: financial information plus courses and grades.

To set it up, the student logs in to Experience USC, chooses the Guest Access service, and adds a guest under "Create a New Guest Access." The guest logs in at usc.edu/oasisguest; USCe.pay and Payment Plan are on the OASIS Financial Information menu. A parent paying from abroad needs guest access before paying on the student's behalf.

Source page: https://sfs.usc.edu/guest-users/
