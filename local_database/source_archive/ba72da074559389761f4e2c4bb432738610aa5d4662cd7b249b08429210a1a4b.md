# 1098-T tax forms, SSN and ITIN for international students at USC

## Tax forms for international students (1098-T, SSN/ITIN)

The IRS Form 1098-T is only useful if you file a U.S. tax return and may qualify for education credits (see IRS Publication 970); if you do not file, you can ignore 1098-T messages. Forms are prepared in January and available in Heartland ECSI by January 31; ECSI emails download instructions. USC cannot give tax advice. To add your SSN or ITIN: log in to Experience USC, click the profile icon > My Profile, click Edit in Personal Info, scroll to My SSN/ITIN, enter the number, and submit by December 31 of the tax year. If added after January 1, the 1098-T is updated within 5 business days.

How to get an SSN or ITIN: Office of International Services (ois.usc.edu/living-in-la/money/). Canadian TL11A form: submit the electronic request; it is emailed within 5-7 business days; questions to cashier@usc.edu.

## USCe.pay Tax Forms: why there is no 1098-T

USCe.pay > Tax Forms lists any 1098-T forms to view or save. If none are available, the usual reasons are: (a) you made no payments during the year, or (b) USC does not have your Social Security number - an SSN is required to generate a 1098-T. Many international students do not have an SSN or do not need a 1098-T; if you get an SSN or ITIN, add it in Experience USC > My Profile > Personal Info > My SSN/ITIN by December 31. Questions: uscsfs@usc.edu.
