# USC immunization, TB screening and consent requirements for new students (2026-2027)

## Required immunizations for new USC students

All new undergraduate and graduate students taking in-person (including hybrid) classes must meet the requirements; online-only students do not. Required: (1) Measles and Mumps (MMR) - proof of two doses of MMR, or a blood titer showing immunity to measles and mumps; anyone born before 1/1/1957 is exempt. (2) Varicella (chickenpox) - proof of two doses, or a blood titer showing immunity; anyone born before 1/1/1980 is exempt. If you had measles, mumps or chickenpox as a child, or cannot find vaccination proof, you must get a blood titer test.

(3) Meningococcal ACWY - for students 21 and under, the most recent dose must be on or after your 16th birthday. Strongly recommended: COVID-19, yearly flu, Tdap within 10 years, Hepatitis A (2 doses), Hepatitis B (2-3 doses), HPV (up to age 45), Meningococcal B (ages 16-23), polio childhood series. If you cannot finish before arriving, attend the Immunizations Fair ("Immu-Fair") in the first week of classes. Missing records after the deadline cause a registration hold.

## How to upload vaccination records to MySHR and what documents are accepted

Log in to MySHR (usc.edu/myshr), go to Medical Records, and upload a copy of your vaccination records. Accepted records must show your full name and date of birth. Screenshots of an official health portal record are accepted. Records not in English must include an English translation. Examples of accepted documents: California immunization card, PRC (China) immunization card or booklet, or a translated immunization record. Lab reports (titers, TB blood tests) must show: patient full name; date of birth; name of the test; date of the test; numeric result values (only "positive"/"negative" is not enough); reference ranges; and the ordering provider's name. Help: 213-740-9355, studenthealth@usc.edu.

Source page: https://sites.usc.edu/new-student-health-requirements/immunizations/uploading-vaccination-records/

## TB (tuberculosis) screening for international students

Students arriving from outside the U.S. may be required to complete a TB test, depending on country criteria published by Student Health (country list spreadsheet on the page). Only blood-draw TB tests (IGRA) are accepted - not skin tests. You must be at least 28 days past any recent MMR, varicella or COVID-19 vaccine before the TB test; Student Health recommends getting the TB test and, if needed, the COVID-19 vaccine on the same day.

A prior TB blood test is accepted only if it was done at a U.S. laboratory within 6 months of the start of classes - so most international students get the TB blood test after arriving, booked through MySHR > Appointments. The lab report must meet the lab report rules (name, DOB, test name, date, numeric values, reference ranges, ordering provider). Incomplete clearances after the deadline lead to a registration hold.

Source page: https://sites.usc.edu/new-student-health-requirements/immunizations/special-clearances-tb-screening/

## Consent for treatment (required form in MySHR)

Every new student must complete the consent for treatment in MySHR before receiving care: log in at usc.edu/myshr (NetID must be active) and go to Consent Forms. Students under 18 need either a parent/guardian to complete the Third-Party Consent to Treatment of a Minor form, or the student completes the Self-Sufficient Minor Status Verification form; both are linked from the Consent for Treatment page.

Source page: https://sites.usc.edu/new-student-health-requirements/home/consent-for-treatment/

## Student Health deadlines: immunizations and insurance decision (by term)

Immunizations should be completed by the first day of classes; students who cannot finish before arriving attend the Immu-Fair in week one. The immunization and insurance decision deadline is the Friday of the third week of classes. Fall 2026: classes start August 24, 2026; deadline September 11, 2026. Spring 2027: classes start January 11, 2027; deadline January 29, 2027. Later insurance decision deadlines: Fall 2027 - September 10, 2027; Spring 2028 - January 28, 2028; Fall 2028 - September 8, 2028; Spring 2029 - January 26, 2029. If immunization records are incomplete at the deadline, a registration hold is placed on your student account.

If you want to use your own insurance you must submit the waiver by the deadline; otherwise the SHIP/Aetna enrollment and its premium on your student account become non-reversible.

Source page: https://studenthealth.usc.edu/fees/
