# IERF degree certification for USC graduate students with foreign degrees

## Why USC requires IERF certification

Some USC admission offers carry a registration requirement: if your degree is from a school outside the United States, you must have the degree certified by the International Education Research Foundation (IERF), a credential evaluation service in Culver City, California, before or during your first semester. IERF evaluates your diploma and transcripts and sends the official report to USC Degree Progress.


## Documents required for IERF certification

1. Original degree certificate in English and/or the original language. 2. Original graduation (completion) certificate in English and/or the original language. 3. Original transcripts in English and/or the original language, sealed in the university's envelopes. 4. If you already hold a doctoral degree: a photocopy of the dissertation's title page, abstract, table of contents and first ten pages. 5. If the name on your degree or graduation certificate differs from your current name: a name change certificate.

After applying online, IERF gives you a Cover Sheet. Print it, write your 10-digit USC ID on it, and mail it together with the documents. Also write your IERF file number on the outside of the envelope (IERF emails the file number after you submit the application).


## IERF mailing addresses

Courier (UPS, FedEx, DHL): IERF, 6133 Bristol Parkway, #300, Culver City, CA 90230, phone 310-258-9451. Regular postal mail: IERF, P.O. Box 3665, Culver City, CA 90231.


## How to apply online at ierf.org: steps 1-3 (basic information, education, purpose)

Step 1 - Basic information: go to https://www.ierf.org/ and click Apply Now. Enter your personal, address and contact information. For "How did you hear about us" select "Educational Institution", then select "University of Southern California Degree Progress" from the list, choose Yes to have the evaluation mailed directly to USC, and enter your 10-digit USC ID.

Step 2 - Education: enter each school - country of study, institution name, city, dates attended (from/to) and the diploma name (in the original language if possible); explain any large gap between finishing the program and receiving the degree (up to 1000 characters).

Step 3 - Purpose of application: main purpose = Education; For Admission To = University (graduate) for master's/PhD students; read the IERF general and country-specific document requirements and tick that you have reviewed them. Report type: choose "General Certification of Studies for USC" ($85) unless USC asked for the "Detail Report with Course Level Identification for USC" ($205).


## How to apply online at ierf.org: steps 4-7 (mailing to USC, rush service, records, payment)

Step 4 - Mailing address (official copy): choose "another recipient" and enter USC: Institution - University of Southern California Degree Progress; Department - Degree Progress Student Services; 700 Childs Way, JHH-010, Los Angeles, CA 90089-0912, United States; phone (213) 740-1330; email degreepr@usc.edu. Delivery of the official copy: Email ($10) is the usual choice; other options are regular mail ($10), domestic secure mailing with tracking ($25), domestic next-day ($40) and international secure mailing ($75). You can also ask IERF to return your original documents to your address or another address.

Step 5 - Select extras: turnaround time 15 business days (no extra fee), 10-day rush ($60), 5-day rush ($85) or 24-hour rush ($200). If your deadline is close, rush service gets results within about 3 weeks overall. Rush fees must be paid by cashier's check, money order or credit card (no personal checks); rush applications or documents received after 12:00 p.m. Pacific count as received the next business day, and the clock starts only when all documents and fees are received.

Step 6 - Submission of records: tick how your records will arrive (originals by you, photocopies, sent directly by your university physically or electronically, or scans emailed later); you may upload scans and add special instructions (up to 1000 characters).

Step 7 - Payment: review the summary (example: General Certification of Studies for USC $85 + mailing/email fees = about $105 USD) and pay by credit card, check or money order.


## Important notes about the IERF guide

Fees and options above come from a student guide with IERF screenshots and may change; confirm current prices on ierf.org and follow the exact instructions in your USC admission letter. If you do not complete this requirement, USC may place a hold on registration. Questions about whether you need IERF: USC Graduate Admissions or Degree Progress (degreepr@usc.edu).
